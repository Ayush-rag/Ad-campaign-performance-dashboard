# Key Insights

1. **Overall:** ₹58.61 lakh spent across 5 platforms returned ₹186.76 lakh in revenue - a blended ROAS of **3.19x** from 7,943 conversions.
2. **Best platform:** Microsoft Ads returned **4.83x** ROAS. **Weakest:** TikTok Ads at **1.52x**, below the 2.0x target.
3. **Money-losing campaigns:** 1 campaign returned less than it cost (Meta Ads - Lookalike - Broad Audience), losing ₹1.44 lakh.
4. **Under-funded winners:** 3 campaigns have ROAS of 4x or more but get only 22% of the budget (best: Google Ads - Search - Brand Keywords at 8.96x).
5. **Creative fatigue:** CTR on Meta Ads - Prospecting - Video fell **35%** from January to June. The creative needs refreshing.
6. **Audience:** The 25-34 age group has the lowest cost per conversion (₹590); 55+ is the most expensive (₹1,207).
7. **Device:** Desktop converts at 3.39% vs 1.39% on mobile, even though mobile gets 63% of spend.
8. **Budget reallocation (estimate):** Moving ₹7.28 lakh from the 'Cut' campaigns to the non-brand 'Scale' campaigns could add about **₹24.37 lakh** in revenue over 6 months at the same total spend.

_Reallocation assumes moved budget earns 75% of the receiving campaign's current ROAS. Brand-keyword search is excluded as a receiver because its traffic is limited by how many people search the brand name._
